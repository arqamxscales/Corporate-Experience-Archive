# Task 1: Professional LinkedIn Announcement

## Objective
Share your official internship acceptance on your professional network.

## What's in this folder
- `caption_draft.md` — a ready-to-use LinkedIn caption with your learning
  goals, the Progree tag, and the required hashtags.

## What you still need to do yourself
This task needs two things I don't have access to:
1. **Your actual Internship Offer Letter** — a personal document Progree
   issues to you directly. I can't create or fabricate this for you.
2. **Posting to your own LinkedIn account** — that requires your login and
   is a public, irreversible action, so it has to come from you.

### Steps to finish this task
1. Wait for / locate your official Offer Letter PDF from Progree.
2. Export or photograph it as a high-resolution image (or use the PDF
   directly if LinkedIn accepts document uploads).
3. Open LinkedIn → **Start a post** → attach the offer letter file.
4. Paste in the caption from `caption_draft.md` (edit the bracketed parts
   to reflect your real goals).
5. Tag **Progree**'s official LinkedIn page (type `@Progree` and select it
   from the dropdown).
6. Publish, then screenshot the live post — that screenshot is your
   submission artifact for this task's report.

## Report note
For your end-of-internship report, include: the screenshot of the
published post, the date posted, and a one-line reflection on what you
hope to learn from the internship.
